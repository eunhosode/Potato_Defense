# Potato_Defense

A **2D side-scrolling defense game** set during a war between the Potato Kingdom and the Sweet Potato Empire.

As commander of the Potato Kingdom, summon potato units, fend off enemy attacks, and lead your army to conquer the Sweet Potato Empire.

## Features

- Spend starch to summon potato units
- Generate starch automatically over time
- Upgrade starch production
- Deploy 10 friendly unit types with distinct abilities
- Fight a variety of sweet potato enemies and bosses
- Face different enemy lineups and spawn patterns in each stage
- Play three story chapters and three sets of special stages

## Story

War breaks out when the Sweet Potato Empire invades the peaceful Potato Kingdom.

The kingdom mobilizes its potato army to repel the invasion, then launches a counterattack to conquer the empire.

### Chapter 1 — The Sweet Potato Empire Invades

The Sweet Potato Empire invades the Potato Kingdom.

The kingdom must defend Potato Castle against Raw Sweet Potatoes, Roasted Sweet Potatoes, Sweet Potato Fries, and Candied Sweet Potatoes as the Sweet Potato General leads the imperial assault.

### Chapter 2 — The Empire's All-Out Offensive

The Sweet Potato Empire reinforces its army and steps up its attacks.

Archers, chargers, priests, and bombers join the imperial forces. The Potato Kingdom responds by deploying Sprout Archers, Stone Potato Shieldbearers, Seed Potato Medics, and Starch Artillery.

The chapter culminates in an all-out assault led by the Sweet Potato Grand General.

### Chapter 3 — The Potato Kingdom Strikes Back

After repelling the invasion, the Potato Kingdom launches its counterattack.

With Skin-On Potato Shieldbearers and Potato Chargers joining its ranks, the potato army advances into imperial territory.

The army breaks through the empire's defenders, including Mace Sweet Potatoes and Sweet Potato Military Bands, before launching an assault on the imperial capital.

## Friendly Units

You can deploy 10 types of potato units.

| Key | Unit | Role and Abilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1` | Potato | Low-cost basic unit |
| `2` | Boiled Potato | High health and reduced fire damage taken |
| `3` | Baked Potato | High damage per hit |
| `4` | Fried Potato | Fast movement and attacks |
| `5` | Sprout Archer | Ranged attacks |
| `6` | Stone Potato Shieldbearer | High health and reduced damage taken |
| `7` | Seed Potato Medic | Heals nearby allies |
| `8` | Starch Artillery | Long-range area damage |
| `9` | Skin-On Potato Shieldbearer | Low-cost defensive unit with knockback resistance |
| `0` | Potato Charger | Deals heavy damage and knocks the target back on its first attack |

New units unlock as you progress through the story chapters. All 10 are available in the special stages.

## Enemies

The Sweet Potato Empire fields troops with a range of abilities.

- **Raw Sweet Potato** — Basic infantry
- **Roasted Sweet Potato** — High-health fighter with fire attacks
- **Sweet Potato Fries** — Fast-moving troops with a high attack speed
- **Candied Sweet Potato** — Has a sugar shield and explodes when it breaks
- **Sweet Potato Archer** — Attacks from long range
- **Sweet Potato Charger** — Rushes forward to close the distance
- **Sweet Potato Priest** — Heals nearby allies
- **Sweet Potato Bomber** — Deals ranged area damage
- **Mace Sweet Potato** — Knocks enemies back after a set number of attacks
- **Sweet Potato Military Band** — Boosts the attack and movement speed of nearby troops

### Bosses

- **Sweet Potato General** — The boss of Chapter 1
- **Sweet Potato Grand General** — The boss of Chapter 2 and a major opponent in the final battle of Chapter 3

When a boss appears, it knocks back the entire potato army.

## Game Mechanics

### Starch

Starch is the resource you spend to summon units.

You generate starch automatically over time and earn extra starch by defeating enemies.

You can also spend starch to upgrade production, allowing you to deploy more troops.

### Combat

Units on both sides move and attack automatically.

Each unit has its own health, attack damage, attack speed, movement speed, range, and special abilities. Choose your units based on the enemy lineup.

### Stage Events

In some stages, reinforcements arrive when the enemy castle reaches a certain health threshold or when a boss appears or is defeated.

In Chapter 3, attacking Sweet Potato Castle can trigger additional waves of defenders.
